Guide to Sanskrit Nominal Paradigms
	1. The paradigms listed here mainly follow Bucknell's Sanskrit Manual. The names therefore correspond to the book mostly. Several distinct paradigms are added for clarity.
	2. Some marked differences can be seen in the marut-vaṇik-like paradigms (mas/fem) which are extended to separate cases. These are treated as the same group under the marut type. The jagat-asṛk-like (neu) can also be seen likewise. Read closely in the Manual, pp. 20-21, to see why you see the names as such.
	3. Note that dvāḥ has its own paradigm, which is slightly different from the marut-ḥ-r.
	4. The numbers given in '#' column correspond to those given by Bucknell. Only nouns have these numbers.
	5. The demonstratives are subsumed under the pronoun type.
	6. When a paradigm is selected, its full declension table will be shown.
	7. The list on the right shows all products sorted by their ending. This can be helpful when you look for a particular ending. When an item is selected, the paradigms will be located.
	8. To see the application of a paradigm to words, open Declension Table Window by the context menu (right-click).
