Guide to Sanskrit Verbal Paradigms
	1. The paradigms listed here mainly follow Roderick Bucknell's Sanskrit Manual. The names shown are those of singular third (prathama) person. If a distinction is needed, a specific letter is added, e.g., M for Middle.
	2. A number of paradigms are also added for clarity, either taken from Madhav Deshpande's Sanskrit Primer (Saṃskṛtasubodhinī) or from the author's speculation.
	3. Simple Future and Conditional do not have their own paradigms because they can be computed from others.
	4. The numbers given in 'Ref' column correspond to those given by Bucknell in tables.
	5. If a paradigm is clicked, its full table is shown at the bottom.
	6. Word list on the right contains all terms rendered, according to the selected criteria. It can be searched using wildcards (? and *). For example, '*āt' will search for all terms ending with 'āt'. When an item in the list is clicked, the relevant paradigm will be located in the table.
	7. All data input here are done manually with great difficulty. If errors are found, please kindly report to the author.
