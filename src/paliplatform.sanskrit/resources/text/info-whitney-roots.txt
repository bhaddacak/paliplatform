Guide to Whitney's Sanskrit roots
	1. The images of the 1885 printed version are taken from archive.org with some color adjustment for size reduction. A better OCR version can be found at GRETIL. The full root list is carefully edited by the author.
	2. As for the book used unfamiliar encoding, here are some noteworthy differences:
	   - n̄ = ṅ
	   - ç = ś
	   - ṅ = ṃ
	3. In the searching, you can enter the standard encoding, but when seeing the content, you have to replace the characters in mind, and ignore all accents.
